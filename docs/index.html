About me

Hi, I'm Rebecca!

I'm a calisthenics coach passionate to help people get stronger, learn new skills and enjoy movement From your first pull-up to your first muscle-up, I build programs that fit your level, your goals and your life.

My mission

To grow calisthenics as a sport and to make calisthenics accessible to everyone, so you can build lasting strength with nothing but minimal equipment and your own body.
